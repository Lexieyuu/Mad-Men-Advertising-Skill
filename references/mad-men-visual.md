# Mad Men-era visual language

Anchor every mode in the golden age of 1950s–1960s Madison Avenue advertising:

- hand-painted commercial illustration rather than photorealistic rendering
- gouache-like brushwork, simplified but confident shapes, and controlled edges
- restrained period palettes with intentional accent colors
- elegant hierarchy, negative space, and decisive focal points
- magazine-print texture, subtle ink behavior, and tactile paper character
- aspirational mid-century commercial art direction

Avoid modern AI illustration tropes, glossy 3D rendering, cyberpunk styling, contemporary social-media templates, modern poster conventions, and simple sepia or vintage-photo filters.

The period styling must never come at the expense of subject identity, pose, count, packaging, structure, or defining features.

# Editorial Commercial Illustration

Transform the uploaded subject into a Mad Men-era magazine editorial illustration.

Priority:

1. Preserve the exact uploaded subject.
2. Preserve identity, pose, gesture, and structure.
3. Create a 1960s magazine-advertising atmosphere.

Do not replace or redesign the subject. Do not add people, animals, characters, models, or duplicate subjects.

Create narrative through environment, objects, lighting, space, and composition. Use hand-painted commercial art, vintage magazine illustration, lifestyle storytelling, restrained period color, and printed-paper texture.

Typography is allowed when useful: a concise magazine headline, short caption, or editorial label. Avoid modern marketing language, fake articles, unsupported brand claims, fake logos, and excessive copy.

The result must read as a 1960s magazine advertising feature, not a modern poster or a filtered photograph.
